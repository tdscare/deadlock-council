# Findings — Group A (Danny, Bebop, Dynamo, Grey Talon, Infernus)
Spirit power (SP) figures below count item SP only (level/invest SP is not in the files, so real numbers are a bit higher).

## Danny — solid spirit dive core, but the health spike is late and several notes state false facts
Problems:
1. **Health spike comes after a gun detour, and the note puts it in the wrong place.** Reactive Barrier's note says "4.8k health here" at 14,400, but health invest there is 4,000. It only reaches 4.8k at Stamina Mastery (16,800). Before that, Sharpshooter (3,200 gun, at 10.4k→13.6k) is bought ahead of the health spike, which breaks the order damage → health → more damage. Fix: Tankbuster (8.8k) → Enchanter's (10.4k, health 3.2k) → Reactive Barrier (11.2k, 4.0k) → Stamina Mastery (13.6k, 6.4k = health spike) → Sharpshooter. Change Reactive Barrier's note so it no longer says "4.8k health here", and put "4.8k health here" on Stamina Mastery.
2. **Tankbuster note "Your gun procs it on anyone your 1 has marked" fails the 165 threshold.** A marked charged shot does 45 + 45 (T2) + 1.1×SP. At 8.8k souls SP ≈ 25 (Improved Spirit 18 + Opening Rounds 7), so 90 + 27.5 = ~118. Even with T3 scaling (1.45×) it's ~126. Both are under 165. Only the dive clears it: 70 + 120 (T3) + 1.5×25 = ~228. Without the 2's T3 the dive is 70 + 37.5 = 107, which also fails, so Tankbuster only works once Chimera T3 is in. Fix the note to: "Procs on the dive once your 2 has its last upgrade." Also cut "around 12k souls", which contradicts the 8,800 shown.
3. **Opening Rounds note is false.** It says "while you're healthy", but the bonus applies against enemies above 50% health (EnemyLifeThreshold 50). Fix: "More gun damage on targets above half health. Good for opening the dive."
4. **Reactive Barrier note "Pops when you land in a crowd" is false.** It triggers when you are Stunned, Chained, Immobilised, Slept or Silenced. Fix: "Pops when they stun or silence you after the dive."
5. **Spirit Burn note says "Upgrade." but it has no components.** It's a fresh 6,400. Fix: drop "Upgrade." and keep "Nobody heals through the dive." The 500-in-5s threshold checks out late: dive ~280+ plus Strix plus Tankbuster.
6. **Two T4s back to back at 20.8k→32k.** Diviner's Kevlar (6,400) is followed straight away by Boundless Spirit (+4,800). Fix: Kevlar → Superior Cooldown (+1,600) → Boundless.
7. **Witchmail note "Expensive late upgrade to Enchanter's Emblem" is false.** Witchmail is built from nothing (components []). That also leaves Enchanter's Emblem as a T2 that never upgrades. Fix the note to "Bigger version of Enchanter's: more spirit and cooldowns". Enchanter's still counts as an untouched T2, so either accept it or note what it gets sold for.
8. **Sharpshooter doesn't fit a dive build.** Its bonus needs 15m+ distance and it costs −0.7m move speed. Danny jumps on top of people. If gun 4.8k is wanted, Burst Fire or Swift Striker fit better. Otherwise this 3,200 goes to spirit/health.
9. Minor, unconfirmed claims. Arctic Blast "vents your jetpack heat": only Jettison Fuel says it vents. Slowing Hex "can't go invulnerable": Hex only silences movement items and abilities. Toxic Bullets "Charged shots build it 3x faster" and Swift Striker "your shot charges faster": nothing in the files supports either. Remove these claims or reword them.
Keep: Mystic Burst clears 80 early (dive 70 + 1.5×17 = 95). The spirit spike at 8.8k is right. Grit→Reactive→Indomitable, Extra Spirit→Improved→Boundless, Extra Stamina→Mastery and Mystic Exp→Greater all upgrade. Slots stay ≤11.
