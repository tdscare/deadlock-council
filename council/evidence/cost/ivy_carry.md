- GAP spirit is Bullet Resist Shredder only (1600, token). Kieran wants every colour at 4.8k on most heroes; this is a decision, not a hard fault.
- WATCH Tesla Bullets at 5.6k (others 5.6k-19.2k): 3200 early for a 15% proc; it is what puts gun at 4.8k @5.6k and upgrades to Capacitor at 17.6k.

## Proposed fix
- Headhunter right after Extra Stamina (end of Early, 9.6k): walked health 4.8k @16.8k (was 14.4k), gun crossings unchanged; or after Stamina Mastery (14.4k): no crossing changes at all.
- Intensifying Magazine: move into Early after Titanic Magazine (walked: gun 4.8k @7.2k instead of 5.6k, health 4.8k @16.0k) or drop it and let Ricochet take the slot earlier.
