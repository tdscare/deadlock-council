## Flags (4)

1. **MED** - Tankbuster: Tankbuster @12000 is the earliest in the set (others 14400-32000); it is 2400 for 50 HP (20.8/k) and only pays on abilities over 165 damage.
   - Minimal fix: Move after Spirit Lifesteal/Boundless (~20-26k), keeping it before Spirit Burn.
2. **MED** - Dispel Magic: Dispel Magic (v3 3200 @15200) gives 10 spirit resist (3.1/k) and 0 HP; the health 4.8k was already reached @9600 and counters live in the Counters box (which does not list it).
   - Minimal fix: Move to Counters (sell Spirit Sap) or replace with a stat item.
3. **LOW** - Rapid Rounds, Swift Striker: Gun invest is a token 1600 (fresh T1 + T2 @16000-16800), sold later for a counter/Escalating Exposure.
   - Minimal fix: Drop or commit.
4. **LOW** - Stamina Mastery: Stamina Mastery +2400 @8800 has no HP.
   - Minimal fix: Optional: Fortitude path.
