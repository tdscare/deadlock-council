# calico_gun: Rizuhble's Catling Gun Calico (Gun Calico)

Cost-efficiency audit. Verdict: **1 flag, 1 colour gap**

walk1 flags: none. 4.8k invest crossings (souls spent): gun @5600, spirit never, health @9600. Core ends 56800 souls, 12 slots.

## Swap / Instead-of / sell-for boxes (re-walked)
- No "Swap"/"Instead of" box in this build.
- Counters sell Melee Lifesteal (health 12000 -> 11200, fine). Rich: Armor Piercer<-Burst Fire (gun +3200), Crippling Headshot<-Melee Lifesteal, Cheat Death<-Silencer (gun -6400, still 35200): spikes keep.

## Flags
- FLAG Melee Lifesteal (T1, 800) is never upgraded and only sold in the optional counters box, so it holds one of the 12 core slots to the end.
- GAP spirit invest is 0. Kieran wants every colour at 4.8k on most heroes; this is a decision, not a hard fault.
- INFO five T4 in a row from 31.2k to 56.8k is allowed (>= 30k).

## Proposed fix
- Lifestrike (+2400, no extra slot) after Point Blank: walked health 8k @19.2k (was 24.8k), 4.8k spikes unchanged, core ends 59200. Or sell Melee Lifesteal for Ricochet and Split Shot for Spellslinger (no cost change).
