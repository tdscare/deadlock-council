## N02 — do two bullet-resist reducers add / max / fraction — INVALID (control contaminated; no result)
Root cause found by diagnostic `diag:eclear1` (plugin.log enemy modifier readout): the enemy bot kept its `upgrade_improved_bullet_armor` item across every `hardreset`. Enemy modifier count: before any give 2 bullet-armour tokens / 9 mods; after `eitem` 2/9; after `eclear` 0/7; after a second `eitem` + `hardreset` 2/9. So `eclear` removes enemy items, `hardreset` does NOT (the doc line saying it does is wrong). Every N02 arm therefore faced an armoured enemy, so the "0%" control was never 0% and arms are not comparable; the earlier observation that N_30 = N_0 is explained by this, not by the item being inert. The ~12 Bullet Resilience the enemy displays is this item (plus anything left over from earlier tests) persisting. Do not use any N02 number. Redesign: `eclear` between arms plus a manipulation check (0% arm must differ from 30% arm before the stacking arms count) and Plated Armor / Metal Skin as separate, realistic armoured-target arms; any 12-copy armour case is synthetic only. A cleanup run (`diag:eclear2`) is queued to leave the enemy item-free.
